What do the axes mean?

Imagine Sony and Bose testing noise-cancellation strength, or Airbus and Boeing testing wing shapes. Here, x is one design choice and y is its measured performance. Teams can share discoveries internally while seeing only part of their rivals’ progress. These are analogies for the lab experiment.

How the prediction works

An LSTM learns dependencies across the sequence of tests. We use 5-fold cross-validation and each player-round's held-out model. A per-scenario cutoff maximizes F1, balancing precision and recall for stopping. Predictions use the history available before the displayed action, starting after 3 own tests. Stopping includes submission and timeout.

## Header

How do design teams explore under uncertainty?

Every test can uncover a better design, but it also costs resources. Competitors face the same trade-off. Explore a lab competition we designed to study these strategic decisions, then see whether a deep learning model predicts each player’s next move.

## What do the axes mean?

Imagine Sony and Bose testing noise-cancellation strength, or Airbus and Boeing testing wing shapes. Here, x is one design choice and y is its measured performance. Teams can share discoveries internally while seeing only part of their rivals’ progress. These are analogies for the lab experiment.

Brand examples are explicitly analogies, not statements that these companies participated or use this exact experiment. A single x represents one simplified design choice, not a complete real-world engineering design vector. The explanation does not claim the experiment measures market share.
